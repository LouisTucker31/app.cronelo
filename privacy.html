Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Cronelo collects, why, who it's shared with, and the rights you have over it. Cronelo is run by Louis Tucker in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for your personal data. If you have any questions, email hello@cronelo.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, your email address and your password. Your password is stored only as a secure hash, so nobody can read it, including us.
  • What you put in Cronelo: your projects, tasks, milestones, phases, dates, notes, tags, templates and profile settings. This includes the names of any people you add to your plans.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong in the app, it sends us the error message, the part of the code it came from, the page address and your browser type, linked to your account. These reports never include your plans.
  • Technical data: like any website, the services that host Cronelo receive your IP address and browser details when you use it, and keep them briefly in their logs for security.

We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and we don't sell or share your data for marketing.

What's kept in your browser

Cronelo keeps a copy of your data, your sign-in session and your preferences (such as dark mode) in your browser's local storage, so the app is fast and keeps working when you go offline. These are needed for the app to work, so we don't ask for consent to use them. Signing out removes your data from that browser.

Why we use your data

  • To provide Cronelo: to create your account, save your work and sync it between your devices, and to send emails about your account, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password. We rely on this being necessary to provide the service you've asked for.
  • To keep Cronelo secure and working: to block automated sign-ups and abuse, and to find and fix errors. We rely on our legitimate interest in running a safe, reliable service.

We only email you about your account. We won't send you marketing emails.

Who we share it with

We use a few trusted services to run Cronelo. They process your data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: hosts the database that stores your account and your work, and handles signing in.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the app, and runs Turnstile, the security check on the sign-in screen, which looks at your browser and connection to tell people from bots.
  • Resend: sends account emails, such as confirming your address or resetting your password, and so receives your email address.
  • jsDelivr: delivers a piece of code the app uses for signing in, and so receives your IP address.

Some of these services may process data outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. We'll also share data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and your work: until you delete your account.
  • Error reports: 90 days.
  • Service logs: kept by our providers for a short time, usually days or weeks.

When you delete your account, your account and everything saved to it are deleted straight away. Copies in backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your data: use Download your data in your profile, or ask us
  • have inaccurate data corrected: you can edit your name and all your work in the app
  • have your data deleted: use Delete account in your profile, or ask us
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your data
  • take your data to another service (data portability)

To use any of these rights, email hello@cronelo.co.uk. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd like the chance to put things right first.

Keeping your data safe

Your data is sent over an encrypted connection, and each account can only read and change its own data. No method is completely secure, though, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

Children

Cronelo isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email.
